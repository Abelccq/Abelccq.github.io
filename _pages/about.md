Hi everyone! I'm Abel Lecocq, an engineering student at ESIR (Université de Rennes), at Master's
level (M1). I mainly study *biomedical signal processing* such as EEG or ECG signals, but also anything that might be closely or remotely related to it, like *medical imaging* or *artificial intelligence* (machine learning, deep learning, etc.).

## Currently
I just finished a research internship on *multimodal emotion recognition* (EEG, video, audio) at Nanjing Medical University, China. My work was focused on designing and evaluating deep learning architectures and especially developing new methods of modality fusion. The idea is to model pre-trained features (5 emotions in the dataset I used) in a hypergraph, then apply tools such as the *sheaf Laplacian* to smooth out its structure of emotions, and, following a fusion, classify the different emotions. You can see more details about it on the [Research](/research/) page!

## What I'm interested in
Personally, I'm very interested in neuroscience, the study of EEG signals, and everything related to it. Whether by reading the latest literature in this field or by carrying out small projects, I'm trying to gain more knowledge and improve my skills with the goal of pursuing a Ph.D. in the coming years. For example, I recently carried a small project in Python to extract spectral and temporal biomarkers (such as variance shifts and Theta/Delta band energy) from clinical pediatric EEGs during seizures (CHB-MIT dataset). Another idea I'd like to carry now is to model critical brain transition during seizure with stochastic differential equations to compare it with my analysis of real signals. More details on the [Portfolio](/portfolio/) page!
