Hi everyone! I'm Abel Lecocq, an engineering student at ESIR (Université de Rennes), at Master's
level (M1). I mainly study *biomedical signal processing* such as EEG or ECG signals, but also anything that might be closely or remotely related to it, like *medical imaging* or *artificial intelligence* (machine learning, deep learning, etc.).

## Currently
I'm doing a research internship on *multimodal emotion recognition* (EEG, video, audio) at Nanjing Medical University, China. My work is focused on designing and evaluating deep learning architectures and especially developing new methods of modality fusion. The idea is to model pre-trained features (5 emotions in the dataset I used) in a graph, then apply *spectral graph theory* tools such as the *Laplacian* to smooth out its structure of emotions, and, following a fusion, classify the different emotions. You can see more details about it on the [Research](/research/) page!

## What I'm interested in
Personally, I'm very interested in neuroscience, the study of EEG signals, and everything related to it. Whether by reading the latest literature in this field or by carrying out small projects, I'm trying to gain more knowledge and improve my skills with the goal of pursuing a Ph.D. in the coming years. Currently, I am working on a small project simulating synchronization dynamics on brain networks using the Kuramoto model. The idea is to build a structural brain network, simulate coupled phase oscillators on its nodes, and study how synchronization emerges as coupling strength increases, in particular the special role played by highly connected "hub" regions in driving global synchrony. This project partly reproduces results from Schmidt et al. (2015, BMC Neuroscience) and envisages new dynamic visualizations of the synchronization process. More details on the [Portfolio](/portfolio/) page!
